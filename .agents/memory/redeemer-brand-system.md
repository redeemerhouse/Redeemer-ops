---
name: Redeemer House brand system
description: Project-wide visual identity requirements for the Recovery Operations application.
---

Every production page and shared workspace surface must use the Redeemer House logo and the coordinating brand system from the approved mockups: deep navy shell, magenta primary actions, warm oat/neutral surfaces, and editorial serif display typography paired with a clean sans-serif utility font.

**Why:** The first production scaffold retained generic Harbor House/teal styling even though the approved Redeemer House mockups established the intended identity. Consistency is part of the product experience, not a page-level enhancement.

**How to apply:** Treat the shared shell and global theme as the source of truth. New pages, dialogs, reports, and resident-facing surfaces must inherit the logo, colors, typography, and interaction accents rather than introducing local branding.
